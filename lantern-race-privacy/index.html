Lantern Race does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data — from adults or from children.

A game for the whole family

Lantern Race is a one-button game played with the TV remote. It is designed for families, and children may play it. For that reason the app is built so that there is nothing to collect: no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no user profiles, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

The app does not ask for a name, an email address, a photo, a location, or any other information about the player. "Player 1", "Player 2" and so on are only colours on the screen; they are not linked to any person.

What is stored, and where

The game saves its progress on the television itself, in the app's private storage: completed levels and stars, collected fireflies, the selected lantern, the daily-course streak, and the recorded flight paths used to show "ghosts" of your best runs. None of this identifies a person.

This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Network access

The game itself does not connect to any server. All graphics, sounds and fonts are included in the app.

The app holds the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions for one reason: Google Play's billing library needs them to offer and verify the optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full version. That exchange happens between your device and Google and carries no information about how the game is played.

Purchases

Lantern Race is free to install. Worlds 1 and 2, Party Race and the Daily Course are free. A single optional one-time purchase unlocks six more worlds and Endless Mode; there are no subscriptions, no random or chance-based purchases (no "loot boxes") and no advertising. Payment is handled entirely by Google — Revoba LLC never sees or receives payment details. Purchases follow Google Play's standard refund policy and family purchase-approval settings.

Children

Lantern Race may be used by children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no advertising, no links that leave the app, no social features and no way to communicate with other people. We designed it to comply with children's privacy laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Parents can control purchases through Google Play's family settings and purchase-approval options.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date. Since the app collects no data, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in practice.
